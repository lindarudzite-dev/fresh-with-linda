# Fresh with Linda

A personal landing page for Linda, an independent Ringana Partner in Latvia, Europe.

## Overview

This is a mobile-first, story-driven landing page designed to connect with US stay-at-home moms interested in Ringana skincare products and the possibility of building an independent business alongside motherhood.

**Key message:** "I wanted to be there for my children. I also wanted something that was mine."

The page is transparent that this is an independent partner's personal website, not an official Ringana site.

## Structure

- **Hero**: Introduction with Linda's portrait and scroll invitation
- **Story**: Three chapters of Linda's personal journey (before motherhood → new baby → Latvia community → early US chapter)
- **Products**: Four featured Ringana products with expandable details
- **Community**: Photo gallery of Fresh Dates and community gatherings
- **Business**: Four-card explanation of what this model offers
- **FAQ**: Accordion with common questions
- **Contact**: WhatsApp and Zoom invitation buttons
- **Footer**: Legal disclosures and privacy statement
